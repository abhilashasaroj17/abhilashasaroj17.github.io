01 / ABOUT

About me

My work connects sensing, simulation, machine learning, and optimization to support better decisions in complex civil infrastructure and transportation systems.

I am a research scientist at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, where I contribute to research spanning transportation digital twins, agentic systems, intelligent signal control, energy-aware mobility, and computer vision. I co-lead technical development in RealTwin and collaborate with researchers across multiple disciplines.

I earned my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering with a minor in Computer Science at Georgia Tech. My broader research vision is to advance computational intelligence for interconnected infrastructure and the communities that depend on it.
